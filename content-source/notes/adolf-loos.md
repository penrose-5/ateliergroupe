*Adolf Loos, architecte des pauvres* examines how housing became central in Loos’ work.

It explores how villa spatial strategies inform collective housing.

In rural contexts, Loos develops a sequence of autonomous spaces forming a *raumplan*.

In the urban context of Vienna, this transforms into a continuous ground condition.

This becomes a mechanism structuring both the building and social relationships.

The work ultimately proposes a new condition of *urban domesticity*.
