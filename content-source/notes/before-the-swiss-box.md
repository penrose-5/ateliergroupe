Before the Swiss Box engages with a specific period in the work of *Herzog & de Meuron* between 1984 and 1987.

Rather than reinforcing the “Swiss box” identity, it examines its origins.

It highlights projects of varying scales that share a common attitude toward the city.

Through layers of diffusion and dissimulation, architectural harshness softens and engages context.

The work proposes an alternative reading of Swiss architectural identity.
